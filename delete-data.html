Delete your Nursing Learning Companion data

Remove a submitted question

  1. Open Your submissions in the app or browser where you submitted the question.
  2. Find the question under Your submissions on this device, choose Withdraw, and confirm.
  3. If you moved devices, use Restore a saved receipt file first. Your private receipt is required to control a submission; keep it private.

Withdrawal removes the question, answer choices, explanation, sources, contributor name and review note from our service immediately. The question is no longer available through our daily question or archive service. It cannot recall copies already saved by readers.

We retain minimal submission and review metadata (such as the submission ID, receipt hash, status and timestamps) to preserve the withdrawal record; these records have no scheduled deletion period. They do not retain your question text or contributor name.

Clear study progress and local receipts

Choose Clear my progress on the study page to remove practice history. To remove cached files and saved receipts, clear this site's data in your browser or delete the app's storage. Withdraw submitted questions first: clearing local data does not withdraw them from the service.

Abuse-prevention records

Daily submission counts use a one-way hash derived from the request IP and UTC day. They expire after two days and are removed during subsequent submissions.

Nursing Learning Companion does not require a user account. Read the privacy policy for details about hosting and optional contributions.
