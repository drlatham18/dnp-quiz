# Nursing Learning Companion — free study listing

Practice nursing foundations, evidence appraisal and quality improvement.

Choose a learning path for RN, BSN, MSN/NP or DNP study. The first free edition
contains 64 original source-linked questions, shuffled answers, explanations,
practice and exam modes, missed-question review and progress saved on the device.
Study offline after the first online load. No account, advertising or patient
record collection. Optional progress export is a local file.

This focused study library is not a complete nursing curriculum, NCLEX or NP
certification preparation, clinical decision support or accredited CE. Content
received an AI-assisted source check, not independent nursing educator approval.
Prescribing and procedure drafts are excluded from this release.
