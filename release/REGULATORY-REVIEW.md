# Educational app deployment review

Reviewed September 28, 2026. Scope: U.S. federal baseline, selected state-law triggers, and observed repository behavior. This is a preliminary research and implementation record, not legal advice, an exhaustive jurisdictional survey, regulatory clearance, or a finding that the app complies with all applicable laws. The operating entity, target states/countries, users’ ages, institutional contracts and future data flows remain to be confirmed with qualified counsel before broader distribution.

## Current product and data flows

- The authorized development destination is the public GitHub Pages preview. Calling it a testing environment or adding `noindex` does not make it private or exempt it from laws. No new destination is authorized by this review.
- Assessment Lab contains static teaching media, fictional report values and multiple-choice exercises. It does not accept patient recordings, scans, symptoms or physiological measurements for analysis. Individual assets retain provenance and processing labels.
- `assessment.js` stores comparison results and category tutorial completion locally. Completion is tied to the tutorial's exact content, is browser-specific and is not proof of identity, legally effective consent, clinical competence or professional approval. Clearing browser data removes it. It is an educational prerequisite, not an access-control or legal-liability mechanism.
- `app.js` stores study progress locally. `review.js` stores draft review notes locally and offers an export. The privacy notice describes local storage, hosted contributions and hosting traffic.
- The entire app is **not offline-only**: `daily.js` requests a daily question from an existing hosted service. `community.js` can send question text, options, rationale, sources, consent flags and an optional name to that service on submission. Private receipts are access credentials. The repository's privacy notice describes IP-derived rate limiting and retention, but this review did not audit the remote service, contracts, logs, deletion behavior or backups. Those claims need verification against that service before broader release.
- An existing `downloads/Nursing-Learning-1.0.apk` is tracked in the repository and a September 28 HEAD request confirmed the GitHub URL returns HTTP 200 with an Android package content type. This work neither rebuilt nor distributed a new native artifact. The current build gate prevents new ungated builds; it cannot revoke historical files or installations. Audit historical distribution against the owner's current release policy before broader release.

## Applicability and actions

| Area | Guidance and current assessment | Action / condition to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FDA medical-device software | FDA lists professional medical training/education software among examples that are not medical devices. Static teaching comparisons appear consistent with that category; this is an inference from current function and claims, not an FDA determination. A disclaimer alone does not resolve classification when actual functionality or promotion supports patient-specific diagnosis or treatment. [FDA non-device examples](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-software-functions-including-mobile-medical-applications/examples-software-functions-are-not-medical-devices), [software policy](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/policy-device-software-functions-and-mobile-medical-applications). | Preserve the education-only purpose in UX and marketing. Reassess before accepting patient inputs, interpreting real scans/sounds, recommending treatment or providing time-critical clinical support. Obtain a documented classification review for such changes. |
| Truthful marketing and consumer protection | FTC guidance requires support for express and implied health/safety claims. A footer cannot neutralize a contradictory overall claim. No nurse review, “clinically validated,” exam-readiness, outcome improvement or endorsement claim should exceed actual evidence. [FTC health-products guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance), [app marketing](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start). | Review store copy, screenshots, testimonials, question accuracy and paid descriptions together. Clearly label simulations, editorial difficulty and unapproved drafts. Do not equate passing an introductory quiz with validation or a waiver of responsibility. |
| HIPAA | Applicability depends on whether the operator acts as a covered entity or business associate and on its relationships and information processing, not simply whether content is about health. Current fictional teaching materials do not by themselves establish HIPAA coverage. A contract to handle PHI for a healthcare organization could change that. [HHS app developer resources](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-apps/index.html), [HHS business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html). | Maintain the prohibition on patient data. Verify the remote submission service and moderation process can handle accidental sensitive submissions appropriately. Assess BAAs, security and permitted use before any institutional PHI workflow; do not advertise HIPAA compliance from this review. |
| FTC Health Breach Notification Rule | The rule covers specified vendors of personal health records and related entities outside HIPAA. Educational progress is not automatically a personal health record, but collecting identifiable health information from multiple sources could change applicability. [FTC compliance guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0), [multi-agency app tool](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/mobile-health-apps-interactive-tool). | Document the actual data inventory, processors and incident-response contacts. Reassess before health profiles, wearable/EHR connections, symptom logs or patient uploads. Determine notification duties if a breach occurs; the absence of HIPAA coverage is not an exemption from other privacy duties. |
| State privacy, security and consumer-health data | State obligations vary by residence, business activity, data and thresholds. Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer-health provisions can reach health data outside HIPAA. A nursing study topic must not automatically be treated as the learner's health condition, but tracking or deriving such inferences changes the analysis. [Washington AG](https://www.atg.wa.gov/protecting-washingtonians-personal-health-data-and-privacy), [Nevada NRS 603A](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-603a.html). | Confirm target jurisdictions and actual tracking before deciding applicability. Review general state privacy, breach, security and consumer-protection laws as well as health-specific laws. Verify notice, access/deletion, consent and vendor duties where applicable. No blanket 50-state clearance is claimed. |
| Children's privacy | COPPA addresses child-directed services collecting personal information and general-audience services with actual knowledge of collection from children under 13. The FTC notes its Rule was amended in 2025. Nursing learners are the apparent audience, but that does not alone settle every use case. [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions). | Confirm audience and contribution handling. Evaluate the current rule before youth-directed use or collecting children's information. Do not collect birth dates solely as an unreviewed workaround. |
| Accessibility | DOJ guidance addresses web accessibility under the ADA. The applicable requirements depend on the entity and context; government/public-school deployments and federal funding/procurement may introduce additional requirements. [DOJ web guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/). | Conduct a keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, zoom and media-alternative audit. Audio descriptions should communicate learning content without pretending text can certify auditory discrimination. This change adds readable instructions and status feedback but is not an accessibility certification. |
| Copyright, media licenses and source rights | Education is not a blanket copyright exception; fair use is fact-specific. [U.S. Copyright Office](https://copyright.gov/fair-use/). | Retain creator, license, source and processing information per asset. Verify redistribution, modification and commercial-use rights before adding media or monetizing. Audit community content for ownership and exam-bank restrictions; citations do not themselves grant a license. |
| Continuing education and professional scope | ANCC describes specific accreditation/approval routes for awarding contact hours. This app has no established authorization to award those credits. [ANCC accreditation process](https://www.nursingworld.org/organizational-programs/accreditation/ncpd/the-application-process/). | Keep completion separate from CE credit, licensure, certification and permission to practice. If credits are planned, obtain the applicable provider/approver arrangement and check nursing-board requirements in target jurisdictions. State professional-practice and liability questions require counsel; the tutorial is not a waiver. |

## Release decisions and remaining evidence

The mandatory first lessons explain education-only use, exclusion from medical responses, potentially exaggerated teaching features and the limits of completion scores. Each of the five Assessment Lab categories requires a separate 3/3 understanding check before comparisons open, including direct links. No patient data, signature, legal name or age is collected by this new step.

Before external distribution, retain evidence for:

1. The owner's verification that three distinct trained nurses approved the **exact current content hash**, including tutorials, questions, distractors, rationales and media. Existing gate requirements remain in force; neither this legal research nor a learner's quiz can satisfy them.
2. A jurisdiction- and business-model-specific review of intended use, claims, terms, privacy, professional scope and liability. This research does not create an additional automated legal approval or claim a legal sign-off.
3. A verified data-flow and vendor inventory for GitHub hosting, the existing community/daily service, logs, retention, withdrawal/deletion, security and any native SDKs. Validate privacy statements against operations, not just source text.
4. Media-license and accessibility audits, plus a review of historical downloadable artifacts and other existing distribution surfaces.
5. A new assessment if subscriptions, accounts, CE credit, institutional records, patient uploads, clinical decision support or international targeting are introduced. App-store requirements are contractual policies, separate from legal compliance; review the destination's current rules when that release is authorized.

No external release is performed or authorized by this document. The current preview remains labeled unapproved and the three-nurse gate remains closed.
