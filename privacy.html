Privacy

This study app has no account, ads, analytics, patient record input, or cloud storage. Selected track, practice totals, and missed-question identifiers stay in local device storage. Use Clear my progress to delete practice data or Export progress to keep a copy.

Offline assets are cached on your device. Web hosting providers receive ordinary network request information when you load or update the app. Following a source link opens that provider’s site under its own privacy policy. No patient information should be entered into study search.
