Privacy

Study practice

Selected track, practice totals, and missed-question identifiers stay in local device storage. Use Clear my progress to delete practice data or Export progress to keep a copy. The app does not use advertising trackers.

Optional community contributions

CSV and JSON files are parsed in your browser. A file is not uploaded as a whole. When you choose Submit, the question, answer choices, explanation, source links, learning level, consent confirmations, and optional contributor name are sent to our hosted review service.

Pending submissions are private to authorized reviewers. Approved questions, explanations, sources, estimated difficulty, and your chosen public nickname may be published in the free daily feature and a future paid archive. If you decline public credit, the question is anonymous to readers.

Your private receipt is stored in this browser and can be downloaded. It is an access key for checking or withdrawing your submission. Withdrawal removes the question content from our service. Minimal status and review metadata may remain; withdrawal cannot recall copies already saved by readers. Submissions otherwise remain until reviewed or withdrawn.

For abuse prevention, the service counts daily submissions using a one-way hash derived from the request IP and UTC day. These rate-limit records expire after two days and are cleared during subsequent submissions. Hosting providers process normal network request data to serve and protect the site.

Your choices

Participation is optional. Do not submit patient information, protected exam material, or anyone else’s private information. Keep receipts somewhere private. Clearing browser data removes local receipts, so download them first if you want to keep control of your submissions.

Offline assets are cached on your device. Source links open external providers under their own privacy policies. The community service uses OpenAI Sites and its hosting infrastructure; the study app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Subscriptions are not currently on sale and the app does not collect payment-card details.
