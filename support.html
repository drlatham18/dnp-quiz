What is in this prototype?

The testing library has 240 study-bank questions: 160 existing source-linked questions and 80 new draft questions. Your selected learning path shows a subset. The Assessment Lab contains 30 comparison lessons and five required safety introductions. These exercises and their scores do not establish clinical competence or exam readiness.

Clinical-content review is separate from site feedback. External release still requires three trained-nurse reviews and owner verification of the exact version.

Your progress and privacy

Progress and review notes stay in your browser. Use Export progress in the study settings or Export review notes in the review workspace to keep a copy. Review notes are tied to the content version; old notes do not approve a changed version.

The site can cache study files after a successful online visit. Optional daily questions and external source links need their services to be available. A daily-service error does not mean the whole site is offline.

No app-store download is needed to test this website. On iPhone, Safari’s Share menu can add it to the Home Screen. Do not enter patient information or anyone’s private details.
