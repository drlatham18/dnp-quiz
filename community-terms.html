Sharing a question

Submit only original material you own or have permission to share. Do not submit patient information, protected exam content, textbook test banks, or another person’s private information.

You retain ownership. By submitting, you grant Mahtal Applied permission to review, edit, reproduce, display, and distribute the question and explanation in Nursing Learning Companion, including free daily features and a future paid archive. Submission does not promise payment, acceptance, or publication.

Questions stay private in the review queue. An editor checks supporting sources, answers, originality, and difficulty before publication. If you choose public credit, your supplied nickname appears with an approved question. Otherwise it is credited anonymously.

Keep your private receipt. It lets you check status and withdraw your question. Withdrawal removes the submitted content from this service; it cannot erase copies someone already saved.

Educational study material is not individualized clinical advice or an exam-readiness guarantee. Verify learning against current professional guidance and your program’s curriculum.
